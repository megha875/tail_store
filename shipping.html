Shipping Policy

Talstore Shipping Policy

At Talstore, we aim to make your online shopping experience simple and convenient. This Shipping Policy explains how orders are processed, shipped and delivered to customers. Please review the information below before placing your order.

1. Order Processing

Once an order is placed successfully, Talstore will begin processing it after order confirmation and payment verification, where applicable. Processing times may vary depending on product availability, order volume and other operational factors. Orders are not necessarily dispatched immediately after placement.

2. Shipping and Delivery Time

Estimated delivery dates or timeframes will be displayed at checkout or communicated through the available order updates. Actual delivery time may depend on the destination, courier service, product availability, weather conditions and other circumstances beyond our control.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before the order is confirmed. Any free-shipping offers may be subject to minimum order values, eligible products, delivery locations or other conditions stated in the relevant offer.

4. Shipping Address

Customers must provide a complete and accurate shipping address, including their name, phone number, house or building number, street, city, state and PIN code. Incorrect or incomplete information may result in delivery delays or unsuccessful delivery attempts. Please contact customer support promptly if you notice an address error after placing an order.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking details may be shared through the contact information provided during checkout or made available through your account, depending on the available website features. Tracking information may take some time to update after dispatch.

6. Delivery Attempts

The delivery partner may contact the customer to complete delivery. Please ensure that someone is available at the delivery address to receive the package. If delivery is unsuccessful, further attempts or other arrangements may depend on the courier partner's process.

7. Delayed or Undelivered Orders

Although Talstore aims to meet the estimated delivery timeframe, delays may occur due to courier operations, weather, public holidays, remote delivery locations or other unforeseen events. If your order has not arrived within the communicated timeframe, please contact customer support with your order number so that the shipment can be checked.

8. Damaged or Incorrect Shipments

If you receive a damaged package, an incorrect product or an incomplete order, contact Talstore customer support as soon as reasonably possible. Please share your order details and photographs of the package or product, where possible. Eligible cases will be reviewed according to our return, replacement and refund policies and applicable law.

9. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery will be available only if offered for the customer's delivery location and order. Applicable payment conditions and any related charges will be shown during checkout. Customers should keep the required payment amount ready when the order is delivered.

10. Order Cancellation

Cancellation requests are subject to the order status and Talstore's cancellation policy. Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be available through the normal process. Any eligible refund will be handled according to the applicable policy and legal requirements.

11. Contact Us

For questions about shipping, tracking, delivery delays or missing orders, please contact Talstore customer support through the contact details provided on our website. Please include your order number to help us assist you.
